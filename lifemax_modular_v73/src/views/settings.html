RULES & DATA

Settings & data

Hard rules when you want them. Data stays in this browser unless you export it.

Appearance

Light mode
Switch between the dark and light appearance. This only changes how the app looks.
Native Nutrition (Beta)
Opt-in Android Nutrition beta. Classic Food stays the default.
Show tab timing (testing)
After each tab change on the native bar, shows how many milliseconds it took from the tap to the new screen being drawn.
Native Overview
Use the Android Overview screen. Turn off to return to the classic Overview.
Native Character preview
Native cards and charts. Logging and edits use their existing screens. Turn off for Classic Character.
Native Log activity (beta)
The native Log screen. Saving uses the same rules and save as the classic form. Turn off for the classic form.
Native Workout plan (beta)
The native Workout plan screen. Ticking a set uses the same rules and save as the classic plan; the guided workout and editing open the classic page. Turn off for the classic plan.
Native Life Director (beta)
The native Today screen: the dial, the day strip and the running order. Sessions, sheets and the other sections open in the classic Life Director. Turn off for the classic screen.

Bottom bar

Pick which tabs show and what order they sit in (use the arrows). Hidden pages are still reachable from links inside the app. Keep at least two.

Navigation

Swipe to switch tabs
Swipe left or right with one finger to move between tabs. Ignored near the screen edges, on scrolling strips, sliders, the cat room and the Life Director.
Swipe animation
The page follows your finger as you swipe, then the next page slides in from the side. Turn this off for plain, instant tab switches.
Bar scrubbing speed
Slide a finger along the bottom bar to run through the tabs. Faster means a shorter slide gets you further.
The Android Back button or gesture closes a popup, then returns to the previous tab. It never closes the app.

Quest effects

Card effects
GPU sparkles, holographic foil and tilt on the quest cards. Turn off for plain cards if your phone gets warm or you want to save battery.
Quest and dice sounds
Card shuffles, deals and flips, coins when you fulfil a quest, and the Luck dice. Also needs Sound on in the main sound setting.
Card deal
The first time you open the Quest Board each day. 3D table lays the cards on a candlelit wooden table in real 3D (skippable). Classic cinematic is the flat version of the same reading. Quick is a short shuffle. Instant just shows the cards.
Card art
Which painted deck your quest cards use. Automatic picks the seasonal deck when one is in season. Cards a deck has no art for use the Classic art.

Guided workout

Workout screen
3D gym walks you from machine to machine with a trainer showing the pace of each rep. Classic is the plain screen. Reduced motion always uses classic.
3D diagnostics
Shows a small readout on the workout screen (frames per second, phase, which movement is playing, any error). Useful when something looks wrong.
Trainer pack
Optionally replace the built-in trainer with a character from a trainer pack file (made with tools/make_trainer.py). The file stays on this device only and nothing from it is part of the app.

Haptics

App updates

New builds come from this app’s GitHub releases. Android asks you to confirm each install.

Tab previews

Home-screen widget

Your Life Director dial on the home screen: today’s plan, a moving hand for now, and what is on or next.

Health sync

Turns your Samsung Health activity into XP automatically, through Android’s Health Connect.

Food database

Automatic backup

Your data is kept on this phone. Pick a folder, such as Drive or Downloads, and a backup file is written there automatically.

Android device backup
Let Android copy all of LifeMax, journal and health entries included, to your Google backup (needs a screen lock) and a new phone. When off, Android's copy of LifeMax is emptied at its next backup. The backup folder works either way.

Notifications

Smart reminders for workouts and planned sessions. Everything is scheduled on this phone.

Profile picture
Cropped to a square. Stays in this browser.

Rewards

Sparks, perks, accent colours, cat toys, streak shields and attribute ascension.

Decay system

After the grace period with no training, the attribute loses XP daily. Skill XP does not decay. Turn this off only if you want a softer game.
