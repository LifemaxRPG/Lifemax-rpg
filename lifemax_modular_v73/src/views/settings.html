RULES & DATA

Settings & data

Hard rules when you want them. Data stays in this browser unless you export it.

Appearance

Light mode
Switch between the dark and light appearance. This only changes how the app looks.

Navigation

Swipe to switch tabs
Swipe left or right with one finger to move between tabs. Ignored near the screen edges, on scrolling strips, sliders, the cat room and the Life Director.
Swipe animation
The page follows your finger as you swipe, then the next page slides in from the side. Turn this off for plain, instant tab switches.
Bar scrubbing speed
Slide a finger along the bottom bar to run through the tabs. Faster means a shorter slide gets you further.
The Android Back button or gesture closes a popup, then returns to the previous tab. It never closes the app.

Haptics

App updates

New builds come from this app’s GitHub releases. Android asks you to confirm each install.

Voice (beta)

Cat graphics

Journal privacy

Tab previews

Small screenshots of each tab, blurred and shown while you scrub the bottom bar.

Home-screen widget

Your Life Director dial on the home screen: today’s plan, a moving hand for now, and what is on or next.

Health sync

Turns your Samsung Health activity into XP automatically, through Android’s Health Connect.

Automatic backup

Your data lives only on this phone. Pick a folder, such as Drive or Downloads, and a backup file is written there automatically.

Notifications

Smart reminders for workouts and planned sessions. Everything is scheduled on this phone.

Character profile

Name, build, and profile picture stay on this device.

Profile picture
Cropped to a square. Stays in this browser.

Rewards

Sparks, perks, accent colours, cat toys, streak shields and attribute ascension.

Decay system

After the grace period with no training, the attribute loses XP daily. Skill XP does not decay. Turn this off only if you want a softer game.
